Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Information stored by the app

When you create an account or use the journal, the app stores the email address and password hash associated with your account, journal entry titles and text, and any mood, tags, category, favorite, or AI-analysis fields you save. Passwords are hashed before they are stored; the app does not store them as plain text.

The app stores account and entry records in its configured database. The site operator manages that database and its hosting. This policy does not promise a particular retention period, backup schedule, or encryption-at-rest configuration.

Optional AI features

If you request AI writing suggestions, sentiment analysis, or wellness insights, the relevant writing and context needed for that request may be sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. Do not submit information to an AI feature unless you are comfortable with that processing. AI output can be inaccurate and is not medical or mental-health advice.

Google's handling of API data is described in the Gemini API terms. Review Google's current terms before using AI features with sensitive content.

Advertising and cookies

This site loads Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your settings and Google's policies. See Google's advertising technologies and our cookie policy.

The app also uses a browser session cookie for account sign-in. You can manage cookies in your browser; blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in from working.

Exports, changes, and deletion

Signed-in users can edit entries and export journal data using the available export formats. The app does not currently provide a self-service account-deletion control. To ask about correcting or deleting account information, use the contact page. Requests are handled by the site operator; no specific response or deletion timeframe is promised here.

Security and sensitive information

Access to journal pages requires an account sign-in, and passwords are stored as hashes. No online service can promise absolute security. Journal entries can contain highly personal information; avoid entering information you would not want stored by this service or processed by an optional provider.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its providers change. The date at the top indicates the latest editorial update. For questions or requests about your information, contact the site through the contact page.
