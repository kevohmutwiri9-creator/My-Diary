About My Diary

What it is

My Diary is a web app for keeping journal entries in an account. Entries can include a title, written content, mood, category, tags, and a favorite marker. The app also provides search, summaries of saved entries, and export options.

Optional AI features can generate writing suggestions or analyze submitted writing. These outputs can be incomplete or incorrect and are not diagnoses, treatment, or professional advice.

Use it at your own pace

There is no required writing schedule or correct length for an entry. You can start with one sentence, use a prompt, or simply record an event. Public sample entries and guides show optional ways to begin.

Privacy and limitations

Journal content can be sensitive. Review the privacy policy before using the app, especially its explanation of AI processing. Avoid submitting information you do not want processed by the service or its providers.

My Diary is a journaling tool, not a medical service. If writing causes distress or you need help with a health concern, stop and contact a qualified professional.
